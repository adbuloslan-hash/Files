# Public OSINT Report Archive

This repository is an organized archive of hourly public-source research reports.

## Scope
Reports cover publicly documented:
- U.S. government and Federal Reserve structure
- U.S. military installation posture at non-sensitive resolution
- Power-grid and data-center developments at regional/non-operational resolution
- U.S. military SATCOM programs and broad orbital regimes
- Nazi-era state killing, the 1934 Business Plot, and fascist/Nazi influence investigations
- Defense/government developments involving China, Israel, Japan, Taiwan, and Australia

## Evidence standard
Modern allegations are tracked as allegations unless independently established. Reports distinguish verified facts, primary records, government responses, independent corroboration, contradictions, and uncertainty.

## Safety boundary
No unauthorized access, network probing, military routing, attack paths, protected infrastructure coordinates, targeting-quality satellite data, vulnerabilities, classified information, or Controlled Unclassified Information.

## Layout
- `reports/hourly/` — timestamped hourly reports
- `reports/reference/` — stable reference reports and indexes
- `methodology/` — sourcing and evidence methodology

Generated as a public-source research archive.
