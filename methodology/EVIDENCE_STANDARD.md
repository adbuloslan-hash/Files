# Evidence Standard

1. Prefer primary official records, statutes, archives, agency reports, budgets, hearings, and inspector-general material.
2. Corroborate consequential claims with independent high-quality sources where possible.
3. Label allegations explicitly.
4. Separate historical fact from analogy or interpretation.
5. Record material government responses and meaningful contradictory evidence.
6. State uncertainty rather than filling evidentiary gaps.
7. Keep military and critical-infrastructure topology conceptual and non-operational.

## Modern state-violence claims
Use an evidence ledger:
- allegation
- event/person
- primary evidence
- official response
- independent corroboration
- contradictions/limitations
- current evidentiary status

A claim of a present-day Nazi regime is not treated as established without evidence supporting that specific claim.
