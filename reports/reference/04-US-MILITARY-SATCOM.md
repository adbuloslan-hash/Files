# U.S. Military SATCOM — Public Architecture

## WGS
Wideband Global SATCOM is publicly described by the U.S. Space Force as a major military wideband communications system supporting U.S. and partner users.

## AEHF
Advanced Extremely High Frequency provides protected, survivable and jam-resistant communications and is publicly described as operating in geosynchronous Earth orbit.

## Milstar
Milstar is an earlier protected military SATCOM architecture using geosynchronous satellites.

## Boundary
This archive does not compile targeting-quality ephemerides, live tracks, terminal coordinates, military frequencies, link budgets, encryption details, interception methods or disruption techniques.
