# Nazi State Killing, U.S. Fascist Influence, and the 1934 Business Plot

## Nazi state killing
Nazi Germany carried out systematic state killing. Aktion T4 targeted institutionalized people with disabilities and became an important precursor to broader Nazi mass murder. Victims were killed through methods including gas, lethal medication and deliberate starvation.

## American fascist/Nazi investigation
In 1934 the U.S. House established the Special Committee on Un-American Activities Authorized to Investigate Nazi Propaganda and Certain Other Propaganda Activities, commonly called the McCormack-Dickstein Committee.

National Archives descriptions document extensive testimony and investigation of organizations associated with Nazi and fascist propaganda and activity in the United States.

## Smedley Butler / Business Plot
Retired Marine Corps Major General Smedley Butler alleged that wealthy interests, acting through intermediaries, approached him concerning leadership of a veterans-based political movement. Elements of his allegations entered congressional proceedings.

The historical record establishes that Butler made these allegations and that Congress investigated fascist/Nazi activities. Stronger claims that a completely developed and imminent coup was conclusively proven remain historically disputed.

## Modern claims
A present-day claim that a government is killing its own population for a Nazi regime requires evidence specific to that claim. This archive does not convert unrelated incidents or political analogies into proof.

Modern allegations are logged using:
1. allegation
2. event/person
3. primary evidence
4. official response
5. independent corroboration
6. contradictory evidence/limitations
7. current evidentiary status
