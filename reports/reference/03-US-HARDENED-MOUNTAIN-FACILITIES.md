# U.S. Mountain and Hardened Facilities — Public History

## Confirmed public examples
Cheyenne Mountain, Colorado, is a publicly acknowledged hardened military complex historically associated with NORAD and aerospace warning missions.

Mount Weather, Virginia, is publicly acknowledged as a federal emergency-management facility associated with continuity and emergency operations.

## Evidentiary rule
There is no reliable public basis for claiming a complete inventory of every sensitive U.S. government mountain facility. Internet lists frequently mix confirmed sites, historical facilities, speculation and unsupported claims.

Operational layouts, access systems, communications architecture and vulnerabilities are excluded.
