# U.S. Federal Government and Federal Reserve — Structural Overview

## Federal government
The constitutional federal government is organized around the legislative, executive and judicial branches. Congress legislates, appropriates and conducts oversight; the executive administers federal law and policy; federal courts adjudicate matters within their jurisdiction.

## Federal Reserve
The Federal Reserve System is the U.S. central bank. Its principal components are:
- Board of Governors
- 12 Federal Reserve Banks
- Federal Open Market Committee

The Board of Governors is a federal agency. Governors are nominated by the President and confirmed by the Senate. The Reserve Banks serve as regional operating arms of the system. The FOMC is the principal monetary-policy body.

The Federal Reserve states that the system is not owned by a person or ordinary private company. Its institutional structure combines a federal Board with congressionally created regional Reserve Banks.
