# China and Israel — Public Defense Structures

## China
China's armed forces operate under the People's Liberation Army and Central Military Commission. Public analysis covers PLA services, theater commands, nuclear forces, space capabilities, cyber/information functions, logistics and modernization.

## Israel
Israel's defense establishment includes the Ministry of Defense and Israel Defense Forces. Public Ministry material describes responsibilities including defense policy, force-building support, budgeting, research and development, procurement and international defense relations.

## Boundary
No military network routes, protected communications topology, live deployments, base vulnerabilities or unauthorized access methods are included.
