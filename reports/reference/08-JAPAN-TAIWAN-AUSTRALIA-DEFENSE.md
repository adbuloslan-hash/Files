# Japan, Taiwan and Australia — Public Defense Structures

## Japan
Japan's Self-Defense Forces comprise Ground, Maritime and Air components. Public U.S.-Japan alliance material describes continuing command-and-control and interoperability reforms.

## Taiwan
Taiwan's defense establishment centers on its Ministry of National Defense and armed forces. Public strategic analysis addresses air and missile defense, maritime defense, ground forces, reserves, command resilience and asymmetric defense.

## Australia
Australia's defense establishment includes the Department of Defence and Australian Defence Force: Royal Australian Navy, Australian Army and Royal Australian Air Force. Public AUSMIN material describes U.S.-Australia force-posture cooperation across land, maritime, air, space, logistics and sustainment.

Operational vulnerabilities and protected routing information are excluded.
