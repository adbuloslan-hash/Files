# U.S. Military Installations Worldwide — Public Overview

## Public baseline
The Department of Defense publicly describes a global real-property portfolio spanning hundreds of installations and locations in more than 30 foreign countries. Detailed current Base Structure Report data may be access-controlled, so this archive relies on public DoD installation directories and official releases.

## Geographic picture
Publicly acknowledged U.S. facilities and activities span Europe, the Indo-Pacific, the Middle East and the Americas. Major publicly documented concentrations include Germany, Italy, the United Kingdom, Japan, the Republic of Korea, Guam, Bahrain, Spain, Belgium and other allied/partner locations.

## Boundary
No protected-site coordinates, security layouts, ammunition/fuel storage details, vulnerabilities, access procedures or attack-relevant inventories are included.
