# U.S. Power Grid and Data Centers — Public Overview

## Grid structure
The U.S. bulk-power system consists of generation, high-voltage transmission, substations, balancing authorities, regional transmission organizations and independent system operators.

## Data-center demand
Large hyperscale and AI data centers are an increasingly important source of regional electricity-load growth. Public planning documents discuss resulting generation, transmission and interconnection requirements.

## Geographic resolution
This archive discusses major public market regions and strategic trends rather than compiling targeting maps of substations, switching stations, control centers, protected government data centers or physical-security weaknesses.
